Employment Classification Questionnaire
📌 Answer all 8 factors carefully. The analysis engine applies the Control Test, Integration Test, Economic Reality Test, and Sham Contract Doctrine as enunciated by the Supreme Court of India. All fields are required.

The "Control Test" (Hussainbhai v. Alath Factory) — supervision over how, when and where work is done is the most critical indicator.

Economic Reality Test — who actually pays, and is it indistinguishable from direct employment?

SAIL v. NUW Waterfront Workers — whether the work is integral to business or peripheral (cleaning, security, catering etc.)

ONGC v. PCL Union — continuous service especially post-240/190 days is strong indicator of disguised employment.

Balwant Rai Saluja v. Air India — workers using PE's tools, premises and working alongside permanent employees signals integration.

Dena Nath v. NFC; BHEL v. Jakhmola — is the contractor an independent enterprise or a paper-entity created to avoid direct employment obligations?

Clearly Sham/Bogus — contractor has no independent existence, no other clients, no separate infrastructure; merely a device to deny regularisation benefits
Probably Sham — contractor has minimal independent operations; exclusively or primarily serves this one principal employer; no genuine entrepreneurial risk
Partially Genuine — contractor has some independent business but relationship with this PE is disproportionately dominant and exclusive in practice
Mostly Genuine — contractor operates independently, has multiple clients, bears genuine entrepreneurial risk and administrative burden
Genuinely Independent — fully independent contractor with own workforce, financial risk, expertise and market presence; contract is a legitimate commercial arrangement

Whether the establishment is registered under CLRA s.7, contractor holds valid licence under s.12, and whether CLRA abolition notification has been issued.

Workmen of Nilgiri Coop. v. State of TN — quality of documentary evidence is critical for both ID proceedings and Writ jurisdiction.
